Rework `node:net` around the new faithful `node:stream` port: `net.Socket` is now a real `stream.Duplex` (function-constructor, callable without `new`) with `_read`/`_write`/`_final`/`_destroy` wired to the `ByteTransport` web streams — backpressure-aware incoming pump (`pause`/`resume`/`pipe`/`unshift`), queued writes while connecting, `bytesRead`/`bytesWritten`, `connecting`/`readyState`/`remoteAddress`/`remotePort`/`remoteFamily`/`localAddress`/`localPort`/`address()`, `setTimeout` idle timer emitting `'timeout'`, `setNoDelay`/`setKeepAlive`, and `ref`/`unref` propagating to a `NetHandleTracker` that hooks process liveness via `__browserContainers.timers` (the loop-backed tracked timers) so live connections keep the worker running. `net.Server` is now a function-constructor `EventEmitter` (callable without `new`) with synchronous `listen` returning `this`, `backlog`/`maxConnections`, `'listening'`/`'connection'`/`'close'`/`'error'` events with real Duplex sockets, `getConnections`, `address()`, `listening`, `ref`/`unref`, and coded errors (`ERR_SERVER_ALREADY_LISTEN`, `ERR_SERVER_NOT_RUNNING`, `EADDRINUSE`). Error mapping produces Node-style errno exceptions (`ECONNREFUSED`/`ENOTFOUND`/`EPIPE`/`ECONNRESET` with `code`/`syscall`/`address`/`port`), `isIP`/`isIPv4`/`isIPv6` are ported from Node's validators, `connect`/`createConnection` accept `(options[, cb])`, `(port[, host][, cb])`, and `"host:port"` forms, and the `NoopTransport` path keeps its documented errors.
